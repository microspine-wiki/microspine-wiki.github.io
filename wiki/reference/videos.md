# Videos

These videos come from the Stanford laboratory's project wikis and were converted for the web. Each also appears on the page that discusses it. They are credited on [Credits and licences](../about/credits.md#from-the-laboratorys-project-wikis).

## Hanging on spines, 2007

![Video of a person holding two ice axes against a pebbly wall, lifting their feet off the ground and hanging while two others watch.](../assets/video/bdml2007-hang-test.mp4)

*One of the first hang tests: a person hangs from a pebbly wall on two hand-held "ice axes" carrying patches of spine toes. 14 seconds. Discussed in [Human-scale prototypes, 2007](../systems/zman-prototypes-2007.md#first-hang-tests-october-2007).*

## Climbing a building, 2008

![Video of the whole climb, sped up four times: the climber moves up the concrete-block wall one paddle at a time, from the crash pad at the bottom to near the top of the building.](../assets/video/bdml2008-building-climb-4x.mp4)

*A professional climber goes up a five-storey building on two spine paddles, loading them through webbing to the feet. Sped up four times and without sound; 3 minutes 18 seconds. Discussed in [Spine paddles on a five-storey wall, 2008](../systems/discovery-paddles.md#the-climb).*

![Video of several people taking turns on the paddles at the foot of the same wall, each standing in the webbing loops and stepping up a few times while others steady and watch.](../assets/video/bdml2008-paddle-trials.mp4)

*Others try the paddles at the foot of the same wall. 5 minutes 39 seconds. Discussed in [Spine paddles on a five-storey wall, 2008](../systems/discovery-paddles.md#the-climb).*

## Making toes, 2009

![Video of a pair of pliers lying on a bench. A hand places a fishhook in the jaws and presses the handles closed with the palm; cut hooks collect on the bench.](../assets/video/bdml2009-trimming-hooks-pliers.mp4)

*Cutting hooks one at a time with pliers. 1 minute 56 seconds. Discussed in [Building spine toes](../fabrication/building-toes.md#trimming-the-hooks).*

![Video of hands holding a stack of white plastic pieces with a line of black hooks along one edge, snipping along the edge with small side cutters.](../assets/video/bdml2009-trimming-hooks-in-row.mp4)

*A faster way to trim hooks, with side cutters. 47 seconds. Discussed in [Building spine toes](../fabrication/building-toes.md#trimming-the-hooks).*

## Gripping, 2009

![Video of a small gripper on top of a brick: a black central box with two white feet on hinged legs. Hands place it, the feet are drawn toward each other, and the gripper is then lifted together with the brick.](../assets/video/bdml2009-opposed-spine-gripper.mp4)

*An early opposed-spine gripper closes on a brick and lifts it. 1 minute 23 seconds. Discussed in [Opposed spines and grippers](../design/opposed-spines.md#an-early-gripper-stanford-2009).*
